# Wallet and prepaid credit

:::caution[You do not need the wallet today]
SpliceRun is in early access and only [Bring your own server](../plans/bring-your-own-server.md)
can be ordered. Every app on it is free, and it does not use the wallet. The wallet is needed
only for the **Shared** plan, which is not open for new orders. Terms: see
[How pricing works](./how-pricing-works.md).
:::

**Shared instances are paid from a prepaid credit wallet** instead of a recurring charge. This
page describes how that works, for anyone who already has one. We do not store your card, and
nothing on your account renews on its own.

Bring your own server and Dedicated do not use the wallet.

## How the wallet works

Your wallet holds a balance. A small daily charge is deducted for each active Shared instance.
The dashboard shows your current balance, the daily charge, and **how many days of runway that
balance represents** - the number to watch.

When the balance reaches zero, your instance is paused until you top it up.

## Topping up

Go to **Wallet** in the left sidebar and click **Top up**. The dashboard shows the minimum and
suggested amounts and any bonus credit on larger top-ups at the time you pay. Prices and
amounts shown there are the current ones; do not rely on a cached page.

## Low-balance warning

When your balance drops below about **seven days of runway**, we email you once and the dashboard
shows a warning. Runway is worked out from what you are actually being charged across every
active Shared instance on the account. Adding credit clears the warning.

## What happens when the balance runs out

Your instance is **paused**: the app is stopped, and your data is kept. Top up and it resumes.
How long paused data is kept before deletion is set out in
[Billing, Cancellation, and Data Policy](./cancellation-data-policy.md); we tell you before
anything is deleted.

## Auto top-up is not available, and that is deliberate

The wallet cannot refill itself, because that would mean us holding a card we can charge without
asking. **We do not store your card, and nothing on your account renews on its own.** To keep
control of every charge, top up by hand when the low-balance warning appears.

## Transaction history

The Wallet page lists every debit and credit on your account, including daily charges, top-ups
and any adjustments. For what happens to money already paid, see the Refunds section of the
[Billing, Cancellation, and Data Policy](./cancellation-data-policy.md#refunds).
