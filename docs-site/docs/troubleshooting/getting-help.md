# Getting help

**Support is through support tickets only.** Open one from your dashboard: **Support**, or
[app.splicerun.com/dashboard/support](https://app.splicerun.com/dashboard/support).

The free plan doesn't include human support. Ticket replies are
best-effort, and we do not guarantee a reply time. Start with these docs; most answers are here.

Email support@splicerun.com only for important matters a ticket can't cover: you can't sign
in, a security report, or a legal or privacy request. Other emails may not get a reply. See
[Contact](#contact).

## How to open a support ticket

1. Sign in to [app.splicerun.com](https://app.splicerun.com).
2. Go to **Support** in the left sidebar, or open
   [app.splicerun.com/dashboard/support](https://app.splicerun.com/dashboard/support).
3. Click **New ticket**.
4. Select the instance the issue is about (this saves a back-and-forth asking for it).
5. Describe what you expected, what actually happened, and when it started.

Every reply stays on the ticket in your dashboard, and a copy is emailed to your account
address. To add something, reply on the ticket, not to the email.

## What we cover on every plan

Platform problems - things on our side - are in scope on every plan. The automatic checks,
backups, updates and certificate renewals run for everyone. A ticket about one of these gets a
best-effort reply:

- Instance not starting or staying up
- Certificate (HTTPS/SSL) errors caused by our infrastructure
- Backup failures or restore errors
- Provisioning stuck or failed
- Billing discrepancies

## App-level support

**App-level work - your workflows, your credentials, your n8n configuration - is
separate from platform support.** We do not guarantee a reply time, and app-level problems are
not triaged on the free plan.

| Plan | App-level support included |
|---|---|
| **Every plan** | None. Start with these docs |
| **You already hold the App-level help add-on** | Your monthly allowance keeps working until you stop it |

Paid app-level help (hourly help and the monthly App-level help allowance) is not offered
to new buyers right now.

If your issue turns out to be on our side, we never charge for it.

## How to get the fastest resolution

The faster we can understand what is happening, the faster we can fix it:

- **Attach the instance** when creating the ticket - it puts the instance ID, plan, and
  recent event log in front of whoever reads it.
- **Give a timeline**: "stopped working at 2pm UTC" is more useful than "broken today".
- **Include the error message**, not just that there is one - paste the full text rather
  than summarising it.
- **Say what you already tried**: if you restarted the instance, or reverted a workflow
  change, say so. It avoids us suggesting the same thing.

## Response times

**We do not guarantee a reply time.** The free plan doesn't include human support, so ticket
replies are best-effort, with no set days or hours.

What runs without a person runs for everyone: the health check and its alerts, nightly backups,
updates in your window, and certificate renewal. Getting back into your own app is self-serve:
see [Reset your n8n password](../apps/n8n/reset-your-password.md).

None of this is a contractual service level with credits attached.

See [what we manage](../plans/what-we-manage.md) for where the line between our side and
yours sits.

## Contact

For support, open a ticket. Email support@splicerun.com only for important matters a ticket
can't cover:

- **You can't sign in**, or you can't reach the dashboard to open a ticket.
- **A security report**, such as a vulnerability or a login you don't recognise.
- **A legal or privacy request**, such as asking for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted.

Other emails may not get a reply. If it is a support question, open a ticket instead.
